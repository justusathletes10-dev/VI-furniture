Field Notes — №07

From the atelier in Batroun.

Each piece begins in a low workshop on the edge of the olive groves, where six craftspeople work to a quiet rhythm — sawing, oiling, joining. Nothing leaves the room until it has been touched by every set of hands.

We work almost entirely with regional materials: cedar and walnut from the Chouf, river stone and travertine from the Bekaa, slumped glass from a small kiln north of Beirut. Slow furniture for a country in a hurry.
